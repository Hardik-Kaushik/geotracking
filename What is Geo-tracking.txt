What is Geo-tracking?
Geo-tracking, or geolocation tracking, refers to the process of identifying and monitoring the real-time location of a device, person, or object using technologies like GPS, cell towers, Wi-Fi, or Bluetooth. It's widely used across industries for navigation, safety, marketing, logistics, and personal use.

How Does It Work?
GPS (Global Positioning System): Uses satellite signals to pinpoint exact coordinates.

Cell Tower Triangulation: Estimates location based on signal strength from nearby towers.

Wi-Fi Positioning: Detects nearby Wi-Fi networks and compares them with known locations.

Bluetooth Beacons: Used in short-range tracking (e.g., indoor navigation).

Common Applications
Navigation Apps like Google Maps or Waze.

Fleet Management in logistics and delivery services.

Fitness Tracking in wearable devices.

Parental Control or employee tracking systems.

Marketing & Personalization through location-based advertisements.

Benefits
Real-time monitoring and updates.

Enhanced safety and security.

Better resource and route optimization.

Targeted customer engagement.

Challenges & Concerns
Privacy Issues: Users often worry about being tracked without consent.

Battery Drain: Continuous tracking can reduce device battery life.

Accuracy Variance: Indoor or rural areas may reduce tracking precision.
